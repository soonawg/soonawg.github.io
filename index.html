Research Interests

My ultimate research goal is to build intelligent robotic agents that can operate robustly, adaptively, and autonomously in complex, real-world environments.

  • RL with Priors: Reinforcement learning becomes sample-inefficient when the agent's degrees of freedom are high. How can we learn effective priors that reduce exploration space, enabling agents to master complex physical tasks?
  • Sim-to-Real Transfer: Simulations provide scalable and safe environments for policy learning, but the reality gap persists. How can we bridge the sim-to-real gap so that policies trained in simulation seamlessly transfer to physical hardware?
  • Humanoids & Vision-based Control: Humanoid morphology combined with egocentric vision provides versatile capabilities. How can we scale vision-guided control policies so humanoids can perceive and act autonomously in unstructured environments?
  • Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models: Integrating multimodal foundation models with lower-level robotic control allows agents to understand human intent and visual scenes. How can we leverage VLA models to achieve generalizable spatial reasoning and real-world task execution?
