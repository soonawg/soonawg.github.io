Research Interests

My research goal is to advance Physical AI by combining real-world robot learning, realistic simulation, and robot foundation models to build agents that can interact robustly and autonomously with the real world.

  • Reinforcement Learning for Dexterous Manipulation: How can robots learn robust, adaptive skills for complex physical tasks through interaction with the real world and scalable reinforcement learning?
  • Real-to-Sim-to-Real Transfer: How can real-world interaction data help build accurate digital twins, so robot behaviors can be developed and refined in simulation before being transferred reliably to physical systems?
  • Humanoids & Vision-based Control: How can vision-based policies enable humanoid robots to perceive, manipulate objects, and act reliably in complex environments?
  • Robot Foundation Models: How can robot foundation models leverage diverse interaction data to perform long-horizon tasks and generalize across environments, objects, and real-world situations?
